## MODULE 1: WEALTH ARCHITECTURE & INCOME STREAM MAPPING

### 1. ENGLISH EDITION (Professional Strategic Guide)

#### Executive Summary
Income mapping within the Indian regulatory frame requires a structural shift from tracking gross revenue to optimizing net retained cash flows. Module 1 establishes the dynamic data ingestion engine for salary structures, business yields, equity distributions, and secondary capital streams.

#### 1. Salary Restructuring & CTC Deconstruction
To maximize tax efficiency under the Old Tax Regime or optimize gross income under the New Tax Regime, your CTC must be mathematically unbundled.

*   **Basic Salary Optimization:** Kept at $40\% - 50\%$ of total CTC. High Basic increases Provident Fund (EPF) accumulation and Gratuity ceiling, but inflates absolute tax liability under the New Tax Regime.
*   **House Rent Allowance (HRA) Engine:** Formula embedded into the template:
    $$\text{HRA Exemption} = \min\left(\text{Actual HRA Received}, \text{Actual Rent Paid} - 10\% \text{ of Basic}, 50\%/40\% \text{ of Basic (Metro/Non-Metro)}\right)$$
*   **Allowance Arbitrage:**
    *   **Special Allowance:** Plug variable; fully taxable.
    *   **Flexible Benefit Plan (FBP):** Food coupons (Section 17(2)), Internet Reimbursement, Fuel Allowances, and Learning & Development Stipends, which remain tax-exempt against valid proof submissions under the Old Regime.

#### 2. Business & Freelance Income: Section 44ADA vs. Section 44AD
For creators, IT consultants, doctors, and legal professionals:

*   **Section 44ADA (Presumptive Taxation for Professionals):** Applicable if gross receipts are $\le ₹75\text{ Lakhs}$ (where cash receipts do not exceed $5\%$). Flat $50\%$ of gross receipts are declared as net profit, fully exempting the remaining $50\%$ from tax without requiring detailed books of accounts.
*   **Section 44AD (Businesses):** Applicable for receipts up to $₹3\text{ Crores}$. Net income presumed at $6\%$ (digital transactions) or $8\%$ (cash transactions).
*   **Template Integration:** Enter gross receipts into the *'Income Matrix Tab'*. The model calculates tax liability using both standard P&L and Presumptive frameworks, auto-recommending the optimal filing strategy.

#### 3. The Hindu Undivided Family (HUF) Tax Shield
A legal entity distinct from an individual, an HUF allows an additional tax exemption slab of $₹3,00,000$ (under New Tax Regime) along with a separate Section 80C/80D allocation.

```
       [ INDIVIDUAL SALARY / CONSULTING INCOME ]

        +----------------+----------------+
        |                                 |

 (Slab: up to 30%)               (Slab: Separate Base)
        |                                 |
 - Primary Salary                 - Rental Income from Ancestral Asset
 - Individual Portfolio           - Secondary Business / Royalties
 - Standard Deductions            - HUF Investments (PPF, ELSS)
```

*   **Implementation Strategy:** Direct ancestral property rent, secondary business profits, or capital gains into the HUF bank account to avoid personal slab compression.

$$\text{Net Worth} = (\text{Liquid Assets} + \text{Semi-Liquid Assets} + \text{Illiquid Assets}) - \text{Total Liabilities}$$

*   **Liquid Assets:** Cash, Savings, Liquid/Arbitrage Funds, Direct Equities.
*   **Semi-Liquid Assets:** PPF, EPF (Subject to withdrawal caps), Gold/SGBs, Debt Funds.
*   **Illiquid Assets:** Primary Real Estate, Private Equity, Motor Vehicles (Depreciating).
*   **Key Health Metric - Solvency Ratio:**
    $$\text{Solvency Ratio} = \frac{\text{Total Net Worth}}{\text{Total Assets}} \quad [\text{Target: } > 0.80]$$

#### 2. Insurance Coverage Gap Analysis Engine

*   **Term Life Insurance Requirement:**
    $$\text{Target Cover} = (20 \times \text{Annual Expenses}) + \text{Total Outstanding Liabilities} + \text{Future Major Milestone Costs} - \text{Existing Liquid Wealth}$$
*   **Health Insurance Floater Top-Up:** Recommends base cover ($₹10-15\text{ Lakhs}$) paired with a high-deductible **Super Top-Up Plan** ($₹50-100\text{ Lakhs}$) to achieve cost-effective health coverage.
